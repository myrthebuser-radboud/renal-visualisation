About this model

This is an example 3D anatomical model. Explore it to learn about the segmented structures. It is for educational purposes only.

Structures

  • Kidney: The kidneys.
  • Simple cyst: A fluid-filled cavity that is often benign.
  • Solid lesion: A solid area within the kidney that requires clinical interpretation.
  • Feeding vessels: Blood vessels supplying the kidney.
  • Draining vessels: Blood vessels carrying blood away from the kidney.

How to interact

Click and drag the main model to rotate it. Use your mouse wheel to zoom. Use the controls on the right to change the visibility and transparency of the segmented structures.
