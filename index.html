About this model

This is an example of a 3D model in a patient that has kidney cancer. Take your time to interact with it.

Structures

  • Kidney: The two kidneys of a patient.
  • Simple cyst: Simple cysts are small, fluid fild cavities that are usually not cancerous.
  • Solid lesion: These are solid structures inside the kidney that are likely a cancer.
  • Feeding vessels: These vessels feed the blood from your heart to your kidneys.
  • Draining vessels: These vessels take the blood from your kidneys to your heart

How to interact

Click on the model and drag your curser to rotate, use the wheel of your mouse to zoom, and use the controls on the right to change the transparency of the components of the model.
